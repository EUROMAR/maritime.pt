Lawyer with over 30 years’ experience in public law, transport and infrastructure, and partner at SRS, where he heads the Administrative, Environment, Transport and Infrastructure departments and the Mozambique Desk. He has advised leading Portuguese port authorities and Portugal’s health PPP programme. He coordinates the Port Law Systematisation Programme across the APLOP countries, chairs ASAP (the Portuguese Association of Law Firms) and the Portuguese section of the UIA, and sits as an arbitrator, including in ICC cases.
