Long-serving maritime executive with more than 25 years in ship owning, ship management, M&A and transformation projects. As Managing Director he led the merger of V.Ships and Norddeutsche Reederei H. Schuldt, and he has held CEO roles at Zeaborn and E.R. Schiffahrt.
