Master mariner and maritime entrepreneur with over 25 years in seafaring, ship registry leadership and maritime compliance. In 2012 he co-founded EUROPEAN MAR (EUROMAR), the exclusive agency of the Madeira International Ship Register (MAR), where he is Managing Partner. He also co-founded OceanScore, a data platform for emissions and carbon compliance.
