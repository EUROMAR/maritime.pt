Lawyer and maritime entrepreneur with over 25 years in ship finance, flag state registration and emissions compliance. After several years in global ship finance at Commerzbank AG, he founded Quarterdeck in 2010, co-founded EUROMAR, the exclusive agency of the Madeira International Ship Register (MAR), in 2012, and co-founded OceanScore in 2020. Both EUROMAR and OceanScore were founded with Jörg Molzahn, and he is Managing Partner at Quarterdeck and EUROMAR.
