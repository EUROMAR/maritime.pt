Maritime executive with over 50 years in shipowning, naval architecture, port development and consulting. He held senior roles at J.J. Henry Co. and at U.S. Lines under Malcom McLean, was Managing Director of Soponata and PSA Sines, and presided over the Sines Port and Logistics Community.
