Shipping executive with over 15 years in dry bulk chartering, shipbroking and ship management. He started with an apprenticeship at Peter Döhle, then worked as a dry cargo broker for Howe Robinson in Hamburg and Tokyo. Since 2022 he has been Managing Director of Peter Döhle Schiffahrts-KG, overseeing ship management for more than 130 vessels and 5,000 seafarers.
