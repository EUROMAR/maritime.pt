Shipping executive with nearly two decades of experience in the maritime industry. After completing an apprenticeship at Peter Döhle, he earned an MSc in Shipping, Trade & Finance from Cass Business School at City University London. He then gained international experience as a dry cargo broker with Howe Robinson in Hamburg and Tokyo. Since 2022, he has been Managing Director of Peter Döhle Schiffahrts-KG, responsible for ship management operations covering more than 130 vessels and 5,000 seafarers.
