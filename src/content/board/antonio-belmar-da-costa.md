Shipping agency executive with several decades in the sector, covering finance, administration and commercial roles in both cargo and cruise agency. He has been Executive Director of AGEPOR, the Portuguese Shipping Agents Association, since 2000. At European level, he has served several terms as President of ECASBA and as FONASBA Vice-President for Europe.
