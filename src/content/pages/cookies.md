updated: '2026-10-29'

This website does not use advertising or tracking cookies, so no cookie banner is required.

- **Statistics** – Cloudflare Web Analytics is cookieless.
- **Videos** – embedded videos only load from YouTube/Vimeo after you click play.
- **Membership form** – the application form on the Membership page is provided by Microsoft Forms, which may set its own cookies (see Microsoft's privacy statement).
