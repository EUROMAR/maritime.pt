## Controller

European International Shipowners' Association of Portugal (EISAP), Quinta São Gonçalo, Rua Nova da Igreja 1, 9060-206 Funchal, Portugal – eisap@portugal-shipowners.com.

## What data we process

- **Contact and membership requests** – name, company, email, phone and your message, used only to reply and to manage membership.
- **Website statistics** – anonymous, cookieless statistics (Cloudflare Web Analytics). No personal profiles are created.

## Legal basis, retention and your rights

Data is processed on the basis of your request or membership (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR) and kept only as long as necessary. You may request access, correction or deletion at any time by emailing us, and you may complain to the Portuguese data protection authority (CNPD).

- Cloudflare, Inc. (anonymous website statistics, if enabled)
